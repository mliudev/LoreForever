# Voice packs for Lore Forever

Everything about narrating and installing voice packs now lives on the website:

- **Narrator guide:** [loreforeverwow.com/voices/guide](https://loreforeverwow.com/voices/guide). What to record,
  recording settings, file names, and where to start.
- **Clip list:** [loreforeverwow.com/voices/clips.csv](https://loreforeverwow.com/voices/clips.csv). The text to
  read and the file name for every narration, rebuilt with each release.
- **Send your recordings:** [loreforeverwow.com/voices/submit](https://loreforeverwow.com/voices/submit). Share a
  folder link, choose your credit line, and agree to the
  [narrator release](https://loreforeverwow.com/voices/release).
- **Installing a voice pack (players):** [loreforeverwow.com/voices](https://loreforeverwow.com/voices#install).
- **Questions:** [Discord](https://loreforeverwow.com/discord?src=voices).

Voice packs are released under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), like the lore text
they read.
