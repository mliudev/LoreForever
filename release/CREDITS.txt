Lore text
---------
The lore text in the files in Data/ is adapted from articles on the Warcraft Wiki
(https://warcraft.wiki.gg), which are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Each entry's "src" field names the wiki article(s) it was adapted from.
The adapted lore text is shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.
