Credits

Lore Forever stands on the work of a lot of people: the wiki writers who gathered Azeroth's history, and the players who translate and narrate it.

The lore

  • The lore text is adapted and rewritten from articles on the Warcraft Wiki (warcraft.wiki.gg), by its contributors, under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • A few entries are adapted from Wowpedia, by its contributors, under CC BY-SA 3.0.
  • Our adapted lore text is shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Every entry names the articles it was adapted from, in the lore data on GitHub and in the add-on's own files.
  • Quest text, names and the rest of the game's own words are the game's, © Blizzard Entertainment.

Translations and voices

Players translate Lore Forever and lend their voices to it. Everyone who has is on Contributors. Community translations and voice packs are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, like the lore text.
