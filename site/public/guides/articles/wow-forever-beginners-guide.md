*New to World of Warcraft: Forever? Here's everything you need to know before your first adventure, from choosing your class to leveling, earning gold, and discovering the world of Azeroth.*

World of Warcraft: Forever launches on **November 4, 2026**, offering players a fresh journey through a reimagined version of the original Azeroth.

Whether you've never played World of Warcraft or you're returning after years away, Forever is designed around something that made the original game memorable: the journey itself.

You'll explore unfamiliar roads, discover stories hidden throughout the world, fight dangerous creatures, complete quests, meet other adventurers, and gradually build a character of your own.

But where should you start?

This beginner's guide explains how WoW Forever works, which class to choose, how leveling works, and what new players should prioritize during their first hours.

## What Is World of Warcraft: Forever?

World of Warcraft: Forever is a new, permanent version of Blizzard's massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG).

It's inspired by the original World of Warcraft but introduces new content, updated classes, expanded character choices, new progression systems, and fresh adventures.

Unlike modern World of Warcraft, where players often focus on reaching the latest endgame as quickly as possible, Forever puts greater emphasis on exploration, character progression, and the experience of living in Azeroth.

The game features:

- A level cap of 60.
- Nine playable classes with updated abilities and talents.
- The new Skyborne race.
- New zones, quests, dungeons, and future raids.
- New systems including Legacy progression and Camping.
- Updated environments and optional visual improvements.
- A world designed to continue growing over time.

Forever also has its own Warcraft timeline, beginning after the events of Warcraft III: Forsaken Kingdom and before the original Molten Core raid.

That distinction matters. Not every story or event from modern WoW has happened in Forever's version of Azeroth.

## When Does WoW Forever Launch, and How Do You Play?

WoW Forever launches globally on **November 4, 2026, at 3:00 p.m. Pacific Standard Time**.

Access to the base game is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time. You do not need to buy a special Forever edition simply to play at launch.

Blizzard also offers optional paid upgrade editions with extra benefits. The new Skyborne race and its Zephras Isle starting experience require the Skyborne Heroic Pack or a higher edition.

To begin your adventure:

1. Create a Battle.net account if you don't already have one.
2. Download and install the Battle.net desktop app.
3. Choose World of Warcraft and install the WoW Forever game version when available.
4. Make sure your subscription or Game Time is active for launch.
5. Create your character and choose how you want to experience Azeroth.

Once you're inside the game, your first quest will introduce you to your character's starting area and the basics of combat, movement, and exploration.

## Choose the Right Ruleset

Before creating your character, you'll need to decide what kind of World of Warcraft experience you want.

Forever replaces the traditional realm-selection process with rulesets that group players around different playstyles.

**Normal:** The best starting choice for most beginners. Focus on exploring, completing quests, joining groups, and learning the game without unwanted player-versus-player combat.

**PvP:** For players who enjoy the additional danger of fighting other players in contested areas.

**Roleplaying:** Ideal if you enjoy character stories, roleplaying, and imagining yourself as part of Azeroth's living world.

**Hardcore:** A special challenge where death has serious consequences. Blizzard plans to introduce this ruleset after launch rather than on day one.

For your first character, we'd generally recommend Normal. If storytelling and roleplaying are your main interests, the Roleplaying ruleset is worth considering.

Remember that players on different rulesets cannot normally group together, so choose the same ruleset as your friends.

## What Is the Best Class for Beginners in WoW Forever?

One of your biggest decisions is choosing your class.

Your class determines your combat abilities, the equipment you use, and the role you can play in groups.

WoW Forever has nine classes:

### Warrior

A heavily armed melee fighter who relies on weapons, armor, and physical attacks. Great for players who enjoy direct combat or want to learn tanking.

### Paladin

A holy warrior who combines melee attacks with protective and healing abilities. Suitable for players who like supporting teammates while fighting on the front lines.

### Hunter

A ranged fighter who can use a pet to help in combat. Hunters are traditionally one of the most approachable choices for solo leveling.

### Rogue

A stealth-focused melee fighter who specializes in surprise attacks, positioning, and controlling enemies.

### Priest

A powerful healer who can also use shadow magic. A strong option for players who enjoy healing and supporting groups.

### Mage

A spellcaster who uses Arcane, Fire, and Frost magic. Mages offer powerful ranged attacks, useful utility, and a distinctive fantasy playstyle.

### Warlock

A dark spellcaster who uses curses, damage-over-time spells, and summoned demons. A good choice for players who enjoy magical combat and having a companion.

### Shaman

A spiritual fighter who draws power from the elements. Shamans can use elemental magic, weapons, and healing abilities.

### Druid

A versatile shapeshifter who can take on different animal forms, combining magic, healing, melee combat, and tanking.

### Which class should you actually choose?

For a brand-new solo player, **Hunter or Warlock are sensible starting choices** because their pets and summoned companions can help manage enemies.

If you love spellcasting, consider Mage. If you want to support teammates, Priest or Paladin might feel more rewarding. If you want to explore several roles on one character, Druid offers unusual flexibility.

However, Forever includes class changes compared with Classic, so old leveling rankings may not perfectly reflect the final release.

Most importantly, don't choose a class only because someone says it is the strongest.

You're going to spend many hours with this character. Choose the fantasy and playstyle that excites you.

## Alliance or Horde: Which Faction Should You Choose?

WoW Forever has two major playable factions: the Alliance and the Horde.

Both offer distinctive starting areas, cities, characters, and storylines.

The Alliance includes races such as Humans, Dwarves, Gnomes, and Night Elves. The Horde includes Orcs, Trolls, Tauren, and the Forsaken.

Forever also introduces the Skyborne, with faction-aligned options and an unusual starting story on Zephras Isle.

Your faction determines which players you can group with, which cities you visit, and many of the adventures you'll encounter.

If your friends are playing, choosing their faction will generally make your experience easier.

Otherwise, choose the faction whose world and identity appeal to you.

## How Leveling Works in WoW Forever

Every new character begins at level 1, and the main leveling journey continues to level 60.

You'll earn experience through activities such as defeating enemies, completing quests, and exploring the world.

When you gain levels, you'll gradually unlock new abilities and become more capable in combat.

Starting at level 10, you also begin investing in your class's talent trees, allowing you to personalize your character's abilities.

But leveling in WoW Forever is not supposed to be only a race toward level 60.

Much of the adventure happens along the way.

You'll travel between settlements, discover new regions, meet faction leaders, encounter dangerous enemies, and gradually understand why the different peoples of Azeroth are fighting.

### Your first ten levels

During your first ten levels, focus on learning the fundamentals:

1. Accept quests in your starting area and read their objectives.
2. Learn how your main combat abilities work.
3. Visit your class trainer and check for available abilities.
4. Keep useful equipment and sell unwanted items to vendors.
5. Learn how to use your map and find nearby settlements.
6. Pay attention to the story that connects your quests.
7. Experiment with your class before worrying about an optimized build.

You don't need the perfect leveling route on your first character.

In fact, getting slightly lost can be part of what makes Azeroth memorable.

## Understanding Quests and the Story of Azeroth

Quests are one of the central activities in WoW Forever.

You might be asked to recover a stolen object, defeat a dangerous creature, investigate a village, or deliver a message across a hostile landscape.

On the surface, these objectives can seem simple.

But they're often connected to larger stories.

Why is a settlement under attack? Who controls the neighboring territory? Why are two factions fighting over an apparently unimportant location?

Knowing the answers makes the world feel much more alive.

For example, Westfall is more than an area filled with enemies. Its ruined farms, poverty, and criminal activity are connected to the political history of Stormwind and the Defias Brotherhood.

Understanding that background completely changes how you experience the zone.

## How to Earn and Save Gold as a Beginner

Gold is the primary in-game currency you'll use for equipment, supplies, training, travel, and other expenses.

During your first levels, you probably won't have much gold. That's normal.

The important thing is learning how to manage what you earn.

Start with a few simple habits:

- Sell unwanted items rather than letting your bags remain full.
- Avoid buying equipment you don't genuinely need.
- Compare vendor prices with player-market opportunities when appropriate.
- Learn a profession that suits your playstyle.
- Keep enough money for useful abilities, supplies, and future expenses.

You don't need to become an expert at farming gold immediately.

Learning to manage your resources makes the early game more comfortable, and you'll discover better earning opportunities as you level.

## What Are Professions in WoW Forever?

Professions let your character gather resources, craft items, and contribute to the game's economy.

For example, you can become a miner, herbalist, blacksmith, alchemist, tailor, or take up other trades.

Forever builds on the original professions system, giving crafting additional importance through updated recipes and the new Camping system.

A profession is more than a method for earning gold. It can also become part of your character's identity.

A wandering herbalist, traveling blacksmith, or experienced alchemist has a natural place in the world of Azeroth.

Choose something that complements how you want to play, rather than worrying about finding the mathematically perfect profession on day one.

## What Is the New Camping System?

Camping is one of Forever's new features.

Players can create campsites around outdoor campfires, where adventurers can rest and benefit from useful features and contributions from professions.

Depending on what players bring to a campsite, it may offer utilities such as profession workspaces, repairs, and temporary buffs.

The system encourages players to interact with the world and one another.

Instead of thinking of every moment between quests as wasted time, a campsite gives you a reason to stop, prepare, and meet other adventurers.

For players who enjoy immersion, Camping could become one of Forever's most interesting additions.

## What Is the Legacy System?

WoW Forever also introduces the Legacy System, an account-wide progression feature designed to reward the adventures you complete across Azeroth.

Legacy includes challenges, perks, and rewards that recognize activities beyond simply acquiring stronger gear.

It encourages exploring the world, completing different activities, and experimenting with additional characters.

However, you don't need to create multiple characters to benefit. Blizzard says character-related advantages can also be unlocked on a single character.

For beginners, the most useful thing to remember is simple: your discoveries and achievements can contribute to a larger journey.

You don't have to rush to understand every Legacy reward during your first few levels.

## Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

Your first character doesn't need to be perfect. Still, a few habits can make the experience more enjoyable.

**Rushing every quest:** It's easy to focus entirely on experience points and miss the stories connecting the world.

**Ignoring class abilities:** Check what you've unlocked and practice using abilities together instead of relying on the same attack indefinitely.

**Spending all your gold:** Early purchases can feel tempting, but keeping a little currency available gives you more flexibility.

**Choosing a class only for rankings:** A supposedly powerful class isn't necessarily enjoyable for every player.

**Trying to learn everything immediately:** WoW has many interconnected systems. Learn them gradually as you encounter them.

**Playing entirely alone when you need help:** Joining other adventurers can make challenging quests and dungeons more approachable.

Above all, remember that learning the game is part of the adventure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is WoW Forever beginner-friendly?

WoW Forever is designed to welcome both new and returning players. However, it retains a more deliberate, Classic-inspired approach to exploration, combat, and leveling than modern WoW. Beginners should expect a learning curve, but they don't need extensive Warcraft knowledge to begin.

### Is WoW Forever free to play?

WoW Forever's base game is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time. Optional paid upgrades offer additional benefits, and certain content, including the Skyborne starting experience, requires a qualifying edition.

### What is the easiest class for beginners?

Hunter and Warlock are traditionally approachable solo choices because they have companions that help in combat. The best choice ultimately depends on the playstyle you enjoy, and Forever's class changes may affect how each class performs.

### Is WoW Forever the same as WoW Classic?

No. Forever is a separate, permanent WoW experience inspired by the original game. It includes new content, redesigned systems, class changes, and its own continuing storyline.

### Do I need to know Warcraft lore before playing?

No. You can learn about Azeroth naturally as you complete quests and explore. If you enjoy understanding the stories behind the world, lore resources and in-game narration can make that discovery more rewarding.

### Are add-ons supported in WoW Forever?

Yes. WoW Forever supports add-ons through its game client. Compatibility depends on the individual add-on and updates to the game, so choose tools specifically maintained for Forever.
