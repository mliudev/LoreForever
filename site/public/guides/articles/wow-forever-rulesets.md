**For most first-time WoW Forever players, choose the Normal ruleset.** It lets you learn quests, dungeons, professions and the world without a setting built around frequent open-world fights between players. Choose **PvP** for danger from the opposing faction, **Roleplaying** if you enjoy acting as your character, and **Hardcore** only once it arrives after launch and you accept lasting consequences for death.

The key change from older realm selection is that Forever starts by asking how you want to play, then places you in that ruleset's community. Blizzard says the rulesets are distinct communities, not traditional individual realms.

## WoW Forever rulesets compared

- **Normal:** questing, dungeons, exploration and optional PvP. Best for most beginners and cooperative groups. Available at the November 4 launch.
- **PvP:** open-world faction conflict in contested territory. Best for players who enjoy danger and world PvP. Available at launch.
- **Roleplaying (RP):** an immersive, character-driven social community. Best for roleplayers and story-focused players. Available at launch.
- **Hardcore:** a high-stakes journey where death has lasting consequences. Best for experienced players. Not available at launch; planned for after.

The real question is not which ruleset gives the best loot, but which community and rules make the game fun for you.

## Normal: the best default for new players

Normal is the baseline ruleset. You focus on leveling, exploring, professions, dungeons and cooperative play, and PvP is something you choose to do. It suits mixed-skill groups of friends, players who want to read stories without interruption, and anyone new to the slower pace of classic-style WoW.

## PvP: the world itself becomes more dangerous

On the PvP ruleset, traveling through contested territory can expose you to hostile players, which changes how you plan routes and whether you travel alone. That can create memorable moments, such as defending an ally or escaping an ambush, and it can frustrate you if you want a peaceful quest chain.

**Important restriction:** Blizzard says that on the PvP ruleset you can create characters for only one faction. If you begin as Alliance there, you cannot create Horde characters on the same PvP ruleset, and the reverse applies too. Agree on a faction with your friends before you commit.

## Roleplaying: experience Azeroth as your character

Roleplaying is about inhabiting a character: a wandering merchant, an anxious apprentice mage, a Forsaken learning about a new life. RP communities value names, stories and social events. You do not have to speak in character constantly or know every piece of lore, but you should respect players who chose the ruleset for that experience.

For lore-focused players, RP is a natural fit, because a quest through Lordaeron means more when you imagine what your character knows about that kingdom.

## Hardcore: a challenge arriving later

Hardcore gives death serious and lasting consequences. Blizzard has said it will arrive after the November 4 launch, not with the first rulesets.

Hardcore stays separate from the others. Blizzard also says a Hardcore character that dies can move to another ruleset, including PvP, instead of resuming Hardcore progress. If this is your first time in WoW, learn on Normal first.

## Can friends on different rulesets play together?

**Not for ordinary quest-group, dungeon or raid activity.** Blizzard says each ruleset is its own space, and cross-ruleset grouping is not supported for those activities.

Battlegrounds are a partial exception. Blizzard says battleground matches may include players from other rulesets, with Hardcore kept separate.

Faction is a second boundary. Horde and Alliance do not group in ordinary Forever content, even on the same ruleset. The safe planning rule is: **same faction and same ruleset if you want to quest and run dungeons together.**

## Can you switch rulesets later?

Not on an existing character. Blizzard says you ordinarily need a new character in the ruleset you want to join. Hardcore has a special death-related transfer path, but it is not a free transfer system. Collections and Legacy points can be shared across Normal, PvP and Roleplaying under Blizzard's announced rules, while Hardcore has stricter one-way conditions.

Settle the choice with your friends and guild before you create a long-term character.

## Which ruleset is most immersive?

Roleplaying is the clearest choice for invented histories and in-character conversation. Normal can be just as immersive if you prefer quiet exploration and story quests. PvP creates intense, unpredictable journeys. No ruleset is best for everyone.

To follow the story while you play, the free **[Lore Forever add-on](/downloads)** offers a lore library and narration where recorded, and it works in game. **[Willo](/willo)** is a separate Windows app for asking questions by voice and is free to start. Neither changes your ruleset or plays your character.

## Five things to decide before creating your character

1. **Who are you playing with?** Agree on the same ruleset.
2. **Which faction will they pick?** Alliance and Horde cannot form ordinary mixed groups.
3. **Do you enjoy open-world PvP?** If not, pick Normal or RP.
4. **Do you want in-character community?** RP may be your home.
5. **Would permanent stakes make the game more or less fun?** If unsure, do not make Hardcore your first experience.

Choosing a ruleset you dislike gives you no advantage. It is a social and gameplay preference, not a test of whether you are a real Warcraft player.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between Normal and PvP in WoW Forever?

Normal focuses on exploration and cooperation, with optional PvP. The PvP ruleset makes contested territory more dangerous because opposing players are part of the challenge.

### Which WoW Forever ruleset is best for beginners?

Normal is the best ruleset for beginners. It lets you learn combat, quests and travel without the added pressure of world PvP.

### Can Normal and Roleplaying characters do dungeons together in WoW Forever?

No. Ordinary cross-ruleset grouping is not supported, so friends who want to quest and run dungeons together should choose the same ruleset and faction.

### Can I play both Horde and Alliance on the PvP ruleset?

No. Blizzard says that on the PvP ruleset you can create characters for only one faction.

### Is Hardcore available on WoW Forever launch day?

No. Blizzard has announced Hardcore as a post-launch addition, so the launch rulesets are Normal, PvP and Roleplaying.

### Can I transfer a character to another WoW Forever ruleset?

Ordinarily no. You create a new character in the ruleset you want. Blizzard has described a separate transfer path for Hardcore characters that die.
