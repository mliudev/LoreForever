**Hunter is the safest first class for most solo players in WoW Forever, with Warlock close behind.** Both bring a combat companion, which gives you room to learn how enemies, quests and positioning work. If you want to tank or heal with friends, Paladin and Druid are the most flexible, and Priest is the clearest pick if healing is what excites you.

This is a beginner-accessibility guide, not a level-60 damage tier list. WoW Forever reworks talents and abilities compared with WoW Classic, and class balance can shift before and after the November 4, 2026 launch. Pick the playstyle you will enjoy first, and use rankings only as a tie-breaker.

## Quick picks by goal

- **First character, mostly solo:** Hunter. Pet support, ranged combat and handy outdoor tools.
- **Dark fantasy and solo spellcasting:** Warlock. A demon companion, curses and damage over time.
- **Durable melee with group options:** Paladin. Armor, Holy magic and protection; can tank, heal or deal damage.
- **Try several roles on one character:** Druid. Shapeshift between caster, healer, melee and tank roles.
- **Pure caster fantasy:** Mage. Arcane, Fire and Frost, with crowd control and conjured supplies.
- **Dedicated healing:** Priest. Holy and Discipline healing, with Shadow as a damage option.
- **Elemental magic and group support:** Shaman. Totems, elemental spells and healing.
- **Straightforward melee or shield tanking:** Warrior. Weapons, armor and Rage.
- **Stealth and precision:** Rogue. Ambushes, careful pulls and control.

**How to read difficulty:** here, "easy" means forgiving when questing alone, not easy to master at the level cap. Any class can feel complex in group content, and gear, professions and the people you play with often matter more than a single ranking.

## The nine classes, explained for new players

### Hunter: the most approachable solo start

A Hunter fights with a ranged weapon, traps and a pet. The pet takes pressure off you while you practice attacking, repositioning and judging a safe pull, which helps when you are learning the difference between one enemy and three arriving at once.

Choose Hunter if you enjoy wilderness exploration, collecting pets and questing on your own. The classic Hunter talent trees were Beast Mastery, Marksmanship and Survival; Forever reworks talents, so check the in-game trees. **Trade-off:** pets need management, and a pet that runs into extra enemies can ruin a pull.

### Warlock: a forgiving pick for dark-magic fans

Warlocks use curses, damage-over-time spells and summoned demons. A demon helps control enemies while your spells work, so the class suits players who like wearing enemies down instead of relying on one burst attack.

Warlock is a good alternative if you like the independence of a Hunter but want to play a spellcaster. **Trade-off:** you manage health, mana, a demon and several damage effects at once.

### Paladin: durability plus room to change roles

Paladins combine weapons, Holy magic, protection and healing. You can quest as a durable melee fighter and later explore tanking or healing in dungeons. Forever adds the Undead (Forsaken) Paladin, one of its headline new race and class combinations.

Choose Paladin if you like protecting teammates and want more than one group role available. **Trade-off:** tanking or supporting a party still takes awareness of the fight; durability does not replace learning encounters.

### Druid: maximum versatility

Druids shift forms to handle different situations. In Classic, Balance covered ranged magic, Restoration covered healing, and Feral covered melee damage and tanking. Druid suits players who want to experiment instead of locking into one playstyle.

It is also an immersive class: crossing a forest as a guardian of nature feels different from fighting through it in plate armor. **Trade-off:** several forms and resource systems take time to learn.

### Mage: the classic wizard

Mages specialize in Arcane, Fire and Frost. Their strengths are ranged damage, crowd control, conjured supplies and travel spells as they become available. Slowing or freezing an enemy before it reaches you often matters more than casting the highest-damage spell.

Choose Mage if you enjoy positioning and clever ability use. **Trade-off:** poor positioning is punished, and mana management matters in long leveling sessions.

### Priest: a clear healer identity

A Priest can protect and restore allies through Holy or Discipline, or take Shadow as a damage path. Players who like helping a team often find healing rewarding because you see the effect of your choices on the group.

Choose Priest because you want to heal, not because someone says the group needs a healer. **Trade-off:** leveling alone and healing dungeons need different habits, so expect to learn both if you want to do both.

### Shaman: the elements in combat

Shamans combine elemental spells, weapon combat, healing and totems. Dwarf Shaman is one of Forever's new race and class combinations. Choose Shaman if you want variety without animal shapeshifting.

**Trade-off:** totems and situational utility add decisions in combat.

### Warrior: weapons, armor and gear upgrades

Warriors fight in melee, build Rage, and can lean toward protection or damage. In a slower, more gear-driven game, a noticeably better weapon is a big part of what makes the class satisfying. Choose Warrior if the straightforward fantasy of a soldier or shield-bearer appeals to you.

**Trade-off:** early solo fights are less forgiving than on a pet class, especially if you pull more enemies than you meant to.

### Rogue: stealth, timing and controlled fights

Rogues rely on stealth, Energy, Combo Points, poisons and control. The class rewards planning: pick your target, decide when to engage and keep an escape route.

Choose Rogue if you like tactical play and a less direct style of combat. **Trade-off:** a new Rogue who takes on several enemies without an exit plan has a rough time.

## Best class for solo leveling

For a first character that levels mostly alone, start with **Hunter or Warlock**. Their companions give you breathing room. A Paladin offers defensive flexibility, a Druid offers several ways to handle danger, and a Mage controls fights well once you understand spacing.

"Fastest leveling" is a different question. It depends on final class tuning, your route, travel time, professions, gear and deaths. The public beta started with a level cap of 20 and was raised to 30, so there is no reliable level 1-to-60 speed ranking yet.

If you are playing for the story, choose the class that makes you curious about the next zone. Minutes saved on a theoretically faster class are not worth much if you dislike playing it for dozens of hours.

## Best class for a group: tank, healer or damage dealer?

Tanks keep enemies focused on themselves and protect the party. **Warrior, Paladin and Druid** are the traditional tanking classes. Healers keep the group alive through dangerous moments, and **Priest, Paladin, Druid and Shaman** are the traditional healers. All nine classes can deal damage, in very different ways. Blizzard has not published a role-by-class list for Forever, so confirm specializations in the launch client.

Mixed roles make dungeons easier to organize, but nobody has to take a role they dislike. If you want to learn to tank or heal, tell your group you are new and ask for patience.

## Which races can play which class?

WoW Forever widens the race and class combinations of the original game. These lists follow Blizzard's race and class announcement.

**Alliance**

- **Human:** Hunter, Mage, Paladin, Priest, Rogue, Warlock, Warrior
- **Dwarf:** Hunter, Paladin, Priest, Rogue, Shaman, Warrior
- **Gnome:** Mage, Priest, Rogue, Warlock, Warrior
- **Night Elf:** Druid, Hunter, Priest, Rogue, Warrior
- **Skyborne (High Order):** Druid, Hunter, Mage, Rogue, Warrior

**Horde**

- **Orc:** Hunter, Mage, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock, Warrior
- **Undead (Forsaken):** Mage, Paladin, Priest, Rogue, Warlock, Warrior
- **Tauren:** Druid, Hunter, Shaman, Warrior
- **Troll:** Hunter, Mage, Priest, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock, Warrior
- **Skyborne (Windshaper):** Druid, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman, Warrior

The combinations Blizzard names as new are Human Hunter, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, Undead Paladin, Gnome Priest and Dwarf Shaman, plus the Skyborne race. The [Skyborne guide](/guides/wow-forever-skyborne) covers that race in detail.

## What changed from WoW Classic?

Forever keeps the nine-class foundation of the original game, reworks talents and abilities, adds new race and class combinations, and keeps the slower, preparation-focused style of classic combat. It is not a copy of modern WoW's specialization systems. Because talents are reworked, an old Classic leveling ranking may not hold, so verify a specific spell or talent in the release build before building a plan around it.

## Which class feels most immersive?

If you want to feel like a character living in Azeroth, class identity matters. A Hunter setting out through the forests of Teldrassil has a different fantasy than a Priest looking into the aftermath of Lordaeron. A Druid may feel at home in Ashenvale, a Rogue in the back streets of Stormwind, and a Shaman near elemental trouble. Classes do not replace the main story, but they change the lens you see it through.

To make leveling more immersive, the free **[Lore Forever add-on](/downloads)** puts readable lore and narrated stories into the game, so you can learn about zones, characters and dungeons without tabbing out to a browser. Narration depends on what has been recorded and on the optional voice packs you install. The add-on is free, needs no account, and runs on Windows and Mac. If you would rather ask questions out loud, **[Willo](/willo)** is an optional Windows 10 or 11 desktop companion that works alongside the add-on. It is free to start, has optional paid plans, and answers questions instead of playing the game for you.

## A five-question class picker

1. **Do you want a pet taking some of the pressure?** Try Hunter or Warlock.
2. **Do you want to heal friends?** Look at Priest, Paladin, Druid or Shaman.
3. **Do you want to tank?** Look at Warrior, Paladin or Druid.
4. **Do you want to move in stealth?** Rogue is the obvious first choice.
5. **Do you love one class fantasy?** Let that settle a close call. Enjoyment lasts longer than any tier list.

If you are torn, make two characters and play their opening zones. In a game built around the journey, a few hours of experimenting is not wasted.

## Our recommendation

If you simply want to start playing, **pick Hunter**. If you love magic and summoned creatures, **pick Warlock**. If you want flexible group roles, **pick Paladin or Druid**. If you already have a strong character fantasy in mind, follow it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the easiest class in WoW Forever?

Hunter is the easiest class in WoW Forever for most new players, because a pet and ranged combat make early questing forgiving. Warlock is a close second for players who prefer spells and demons. This is a beginner recommendation, not a statement about level-60 damage.

### Is Hunter or Warlock better for solo players in WoW Forever?

Hunter is simpler for most beginners because it asks you to manage fewer resources, while Warlock asks you to juggle spells, mana, health and a demon. Both classes can level alone, so the choice comes down to an animal companion or a demon-summoning caster.

### Which classes can heal in WoW Forever?

Priest, Paladin, Druid and Shaman are the traditional healing classes in WoW Forever. Priest is the most dedicated healer, while the other three mix healing with damage or tanking.

### Which classes can tank in WoW Forever?

Warrior, Paladin and Druid are the traditional tanking classes in WoW Forever. Forever reworks talents, and Blizzard has not published a role-by-class list, so confirm specializations in the launch client.

### Which races can be Paladins in WoW Forever?

Humans, Dwarves and Undead (Forsaken) can be Paladins in WoW Forever, according to Blizzard's race and class list. The Undead Paladin is a new combination that the original game did not have.

### What is the best DPS class at level 60 in WoW Forever?

There is no reliable answer yet. The public beta was capped below level 60, and class tuning can change at launch, so be cautious about any ranking that presents exact damage percentages as settled fact.

### Can I change my class later in WoW Forever?

Blizzard has not announced a way to change the class of an existing character. To try another class, create a new character. Legacy progression and collections are account-wide, so experimenting is not a complete restart.
