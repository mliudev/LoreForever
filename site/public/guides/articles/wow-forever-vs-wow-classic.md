**WoW Classic lets you revisit older versions of World of Warcraft, while WoW Forever builds new, continuing adventures on the foundation of original Azeroth.** Forever keeps the level cap at 60 and the focus on travel, preparation and social play, and adds zones, quests, dungeons, class updates and account-wide progression.

Blizzard describes Forever as a permanent experience rather than a limited season or another Classic version. This comparison is mainly with **WoW Classic Era**, the persistent original-style game. Other Classic progression and seasonal versions are separate products, so their systems can differ.

## WoW Forever vs Classic at a glance

- **Overall idea:** Classic Era preserves the original-style game. Forever expands original Azeroth permanently.
- **Level cap:** 60 in both.
- **World:** Classic Era has the established regions. Forever keeps them and adds new and reworked areas.
- **Questing:** Forever adds more than 1,000 new quests across the 1–60 journey.
- **Races:** Forever adds the Skyborne, which requires a paid upgrade.
- **Classes:** Nine classes in both, with reworked abilities and talents in Forever.
- **Race and class combinations:** Forever adds several, including the Undead Paladin.
- **Dungeons:** Forever adds nine new dungeons to the original ones.
- **Progression:** Forever adds the account-wide Legacy system.
- **Outdoor social play:** Forever adds Camping, which links campfires to professions.
- **Starting choice:** Forever uses rulesets (Normal, PvP and Roleplaying, with Hardcore later) instead of picking a realm.
- **Look and input:** Forever adds visual presets, HD and SD character models, opt-in transmog and official gamepad support.

This describes announced features before launch. Beta details can still change.

## 1. Forever is designed to keep growing

Classic Era appeals because its game stays recognizable. Forever promises something different: new adventures built on that foundation over time, without treating the first release as a temporary experiment.

Blizzard's announced roadmap includes two new raids on Mount Hyjal that unlock on December 9 (the 10-player Barrow Deeps and the 20-player Hyjal Summit), plus more raids, dungeons, PvP updates, world content, quests and a revamped iconic raid after launch. Blizzard has also announced a new 15 vs 15 battleground, the Darkspear Islands. If you are deciding whether to invest hundreds of hours in a new character, this is the main reason to pick Forever.

## 2. The world starts familiar, but new routes open

The Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor remain central. Forever reaches into previously unexplored or underdeveloped places, including **Zephras Isle, the Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Mount Hyjal**. The goal is to enrich the space between familiar places, not replace them.

For a returning player that means you can recognize the road without knowing what lies beyond the next turn. For a new player, the additions should feel like one world rather than a string of disconnected expansions.

## 3. More than 1,000 new quests expand leveling

Blizzard has announced more than one thousand new quests across the 1–60 journey. That does not make every old route useless or mean everyone follows one optimal path. It means more places to explore and more story threads on the way to level 60. The Riverglades alone brings more than 150 new quests for roughly the mid-30s to mid-40s.

The fastest route cannot be proven from a pre-launch beta capped at level 30, so expect player routes to change once live leveling data exists. The [leveling guide](/guides/wow-forever-leveling-guide) has a zone-by-zone framework.

## 4. A new playable race: Skyborne

Skyborne start on **Zephras Isle**, a new level 1–12 starting region. The race has two faction traditions: Horde Windshapers and Alliance High Order Skyborne. Both can be Warrior, Hunter, Rogue or Druid. The Horde side adds Shaman, and the Alliance side adds Mage.

**Important:** the Skyborne race and the Zephras Isle starting experience require the Skyborne Heroic Pack or a higher edition. An ordinary subscription or Game Time gives base access to Forever, not this race. See the [Skyborne guide](/guides/wow-forever-skyborne).

## 5. Nine classes, with reworked gameplay

Forever keeps the nine-class lineup: Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Mage, Warlock, Shaman and Druid. Talents and abilities are updated while the classic class identities stay. That makes class guides worth rereading even if you played Classic for years. Our [class comparison](/guides/wow-forever-best-class-for-beginners) covers the beginner picks.

## 6. More race and class combinations

Classic's race and class restrictions were part of its identity. Forever adds **Human Hunter, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, Undead Paladin, Gnome Priest and Dwarf Shaman**, plus the Skyborne options.

This is not only mechanical. An Undead (Forsaken) Paladin suggests character stories and tensions that a traditional Alliance Paladin does not. If you enjoy roleplay, the new combinations are a strong reason to try Forever.

## 7. Nine new dungeons

Blizzard has announced nine new dungeons: Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands, City of Dalaran, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace. Blizzard has confirmed level ranges only for the first two, Hall of Thanes at levels 13–18 and Ruins of Lordaeron at levels 15–20.

The full list, with locations and likely level bands, is in the [dungeons guide](/guides/wow-forever-dungeons).

## 8. Legacy rewards more than one character's path

Legacy is a new account-wide progression system for broader adventure and character activity. Blizzard highlights players who love creating alternate characters, and also says one-character players can unlock character-related advantages.

For newcomers, this can make trying another class feel less like starting over. For veterans, it adds long-term goals beyond one raid or one piece of gear.

## 9. Camping adds a social layer to the outdoors

Camping lets adventurers craft campfires in the outdoor world, where other players can stop by, contribute through professions and share campsite buffs. It may sound small next to a raid, but it matches Forever's philosophy of giving players reasons to pause, prepare and meet each other outside cities and dungeons. Exact buffs and the best uses are worth revisiting after launch.

## 10. Rulesets replace the realm decision

Instead of picking a realm, you pick a **ruleset**: Normal, PvP or Roleplaying, with Hardcore arriving after launch. Blizzard describes each as a broad player ecosystem rather than a traditional realm.

Cross-ruleset grouping for dungeons and raids is not supported, and Alliance and Horde characters cannot group together. That makes the ruleset choice important if you want to play with specific friends. The [rulesets guide](/guides/wow-forever-rulesets) explains each one.

## 11. Travel and danger stay important

Forever keeps a world with **no flying mounts and no level scaling**. Geography, danger and the journey between places still matter, and you cannot count on zones adjusting to your level as you walk in. Preparation, class tools and cooperation stay valuable, and a long journey can still be part of the adventure instead of a commute.

## 12. More visual choice without dropping the classic look

Forever includes modern and Classic visual presets, HD and SD character models, restored Classic animations for HD models, opt-in transmog, rendering improvements such as rivers in Elwynn Forest, and official gamepad support. Players choose how the world looks instead of everyone getting one modern presentation, and the game is not visually identical to Classic.

## Which version should you play?

**Choose Classic Era** if you want the preserved original-style experience, familiar progression and an established community around that specific game. **Choose Forever** if you want the older world's scale and danger plus new quests, stories, places and systems that keep growing.

If you are coming from modern WoW and want a slower journey, Forever is a natural entry point. If you are rejoining friends, play the version they actually play. The best version on paper is not much fun if your group is somewhere else.

## Follow the new stories without losing immersion

One of Forever's strengths is unfamiliar stories in familiar landscapes. The free **[Lore Forever add-on](/downloads)** lets you read about zones, characters and dungeons, with narration where recorded, without tabbing out of the game. It is free, needs no account, and runs on Windows and Mac. If you prefer asking questions while you play, **[Willo](/willo)** is an optional Windows 10 or 11 desktop companion that works alongside the add-on. It offers voice conversations, is free to start, has optional paid plans, and does not play your character.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is WoW Forever the same as Classic Plus?

WoW Forever resembles the long-discussed "Classic+" idea of expanding original Azeroth, but Blizzard presents it as its own permanent experience, not a Classic patch or season.

### Is WoW Forever replacing WoW Classic?

Blizzard has not announced that WoW Forever replaces WoW Classic. They are separate products with different goals, so choose the version you want to play.

### Is WoW Forever free if I already subscribe?

Base access to WoW Forever is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time. Optional upgrades, including the Skyborne Heroic Pack needed to play the Skyborne race, are sold separately.

### Does WoW Forever have the same level cap as Classic?

Yes. WoW Forever keeps the 1–60 leveling journey, with new content added inside that world instead of a rising level cap.

### Will I have to relearn my class in WoW Forever?

Partly. Abilities and talents have changed, so review your class before you play. The identity of each of the nine classes stays recognizable.

### Does WoW Forever have flying mounts?

No. Blizzard has said Forever's design has no flying mounts and no level scaling.
