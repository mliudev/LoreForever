Refunds and cancellations

Updated 2026-10-12

How to cancel a plan, and when you get money back. The short version: cancel any time, keep what you've paid for, and if something went wrong, tell us and we'll make it right.

Plans

  • Cancel any time under Manage billing on Plans (Stripe's billing portal). Your plan stays until the end of the month or year you've paid for, then stops instead of renewing.
  • No refunds for the rest of a period you've started, except where the law gives you one.
  • In the EU and the UK, the law may give you 14 days to change your mind about a digital service. Stripe sells plans as the merchant of record, and handles that.
  • Switching plans is under Manage billing too. Stripe works out the difference for the time left and puts it on your next bill.

Top-ups

  • Unused top-ups can be refunded within 14 days of buying them: ask on Discord.
  • Once you've used some of a top-up's answers, it can't be refunded.

If something went wrong

Charged twice, or paid for something that didn't work? Tell us on Discord and we'll make it right. Stripe sells plans and top-ups as the merchant of record, so it can also refund you.

Deleting your account

Delete my account, on your account page, ends your plan straight away, and any top-up answers you have left go with it. There's no refund for the time or answers left, so if you only want to stop paying, cancel under Manage billing instead.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. The rest of the rules are in the Terms.
