Willo downloads: copies of open models, and who made them
=========================================================

The files in this folder are the one-time downloads Willo makes on your PC (its voice, its ears and its search of the
lore). They are byte-for-byte the files their makers publish, unchanged; they are kept here so a first run does not
depend on another site being up. Each file's address is its SHA-256, and Willo checks it before using the file.
Thank you to everyone who made and shared them.

  kokoro-onnx, by thewh1teagle: MIT License. https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
