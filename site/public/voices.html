Installing a voice
  1. Download the voice's zip and unzip it. Inside is a folder named LoreForever_Voice_<Name>.
  2. Put that folder in World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns, next to LoreForever.
  3. Restart the game (a /reload isn't enough), then pick the voice under Options › AddOns › Lore Forever › Narration voice and press Preview.

A voice that hasn't recorded a story yet hands it to the default narrator. To go back, pick Default; to remove a voice, delete its folder. Prefer no recordings at all? Pick Game voice only.

Make a voice

Want to narrate the stories of Azeroth? Start with a few places; the default narrator covers the rest. We build your pack, listen to it, and list it here with your name on it.

  1. Read the narrator guide.
  2. Record the lines you like in a recording app (Audacity is free) with a decent microphone in a quiet room, and export MP3 or WAV.
  3. Open the upload page: it lists every line with the words to read. Sign in with Google and drop each file on its line.
  4. Send your voice for review from the same page.
