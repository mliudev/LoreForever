How to install an extra pack
  1. Unzip it into your AddOns folder, World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns, so it sits next to the LoreForever folder, not inside it.
  2. Type /reload in game, or start the game if it's closed.
  3. Pick the voice under Options › AddOns › Lore Forever › Narration voices, or type /lore voice. Alliance and Horde lands play by themselves.

CurseForge and Setup do all of this for you. Not showing up? Watch for a doubled folder like AddOns\LoreForever_Voice_Female\LoreForever_Voice_Female\, and on the character screen tick AddOns › Load out of date AddOns. A voice that hasn't recorded a story hands it to the next voice in your order. Still stuck? Ask on Discord.
