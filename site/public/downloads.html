Narrator voices

Lore Forever comes with the male narrator. Every other voice is its own download: get the ones you want, unzip them next to Lore Forever and restart the game. How

Pick a voice in game under Options › AddOns › Lore Forever › Narration voices, or type /lore voice. Install several and put them in your order. Profiles and likes: all voices.

How to install a voice
  1. Unzip it into your AddOns folder, World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns, so it sits next to the LoreForever folder, not inside it.
  2. Restart the game. A /reload doesn't pick up a new folder.
  3. Pick the voice under Options › AddOns › Lore Forever › Narration voices. Alliance and Horde lands play by themselves.

Not showing up? Watch for a doubled folder like AddOns\LoreForever_Voice_Female\LoreForever_Voice_Female\, and on the character screen tick AddOns › Load out of date AddOns. Still stuck? Ask on Discord.
