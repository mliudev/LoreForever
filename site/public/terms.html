What's free

The add-on, every voice pack and every language, this site and player profiles are free, and they stay free. Blizzard's rules say add-ons must be free. Willo, the companion, answers for free too: 30 answers to start, then 20 a day.

What's paid

What's for sale is optional: plans that give Willo more answers a day and more it can do, and one-time top-ups of more answers. A plan renews until you cancel it. Prices are in US dollars.

  • Hero: $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year.
  • Champion: $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year.
  • Legend: $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year.
  • Top-up: 300 live answers for $2.99, paid once. Top-up answers never expire, and they're used after the day's answers from your plan run out. You can buy up to 3 a month.

An account gets at most 200 live answers a day, plan, starter and top-ups together. What each plan includes is on Plans; a plan marked "Coming soon" there can't be bought yet. If free answers get very busy in a month, the free plan's daily answers may drop until the month ends.

Paying

  • Checkout: payments go through Stripe, on Stripe's own pages. Stripe is the merchant of record (Stripe Managed Payments): it takes the payment, handles sales tax and VAT, and sends your receipt. We never see your card.
  • Prices include tax, so you pay what's listed.
  • Renewal: a plan renews by itself each month or year until you cancel it.
  • Cancelling: any time, under Manage billing on Plans (Stripe's billing portal). Your plan stays until the end of the period you've paid for, then stops.
  • Refunds: see Refunds and cancellations.

Changes to plans and prices

We may change the plans or their prices. We'll tell you first, and a subscription you already have keeps its price until it renews after we've told you.

Willo's answers

Willo's live answers are written by an AI model. They can be wrong, and they aren't official game information.

What you write

On a plan, you can tell Willo how to talk to you, and write your own tales of the game's characters, on Willo, your way. We check what you write before Willo uses it. It must not be:

  • sexual;
  • hateful, or harassing anyone;
  • about real people, living or dead;
  • encouraging self-harm or suicide;
  • extremist;
  • a way to get around Willo's own rules.

We may refuse or remove text that breaks these rules, and close accounts that break them.

Your account
