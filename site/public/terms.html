What's free

The add-on, every voice pack and every language, this site and player profiles are free, and they stay free. Blizzard's rules say add-ons must be free. What's paid

What's for sale is optional: plans that give Willo more answers, her voice when you ask out loud, and more she can do, and a one-time pack of Willo's finest voice. A plan renews until you cancel it. Prices are in US dollars.

  • Hero: $4.99 a month. 900 answers a month, up to 100 a day, and when you ask out loud, Willo answers in her studio voice.
  • Champion: $9.99 a month. 1,900 answers a month, up to 200 a day, in Willo's studio voices: you pick which.
  • Legend: $19.99 a month. 4,000 answers a month, up to 300 a day, and Willo's finest voice for your first 1,900 replies each month, then her studio voice; you pick which voice.
  • Willo's finest voice: 400 replies in Willo's finest voice for $2.99, paid once, with any plan. They never expire, they're used after your plan's own finest replies, and they add no answers. You can buy up to 3 a month.

A spoken voice can step down for a while: when a month's spoken replies would cost more than your plan brings, or a voice service is slow or down, Willo answers in a simpler voice (her finest, then her studio voice, then her own voice made on your PC) until that one is back. Your answers keep coming either way.

Answers aren't sold on their own: once your plan's answers for the day or the month are used, Willo answers from the add-on's lore library until they come back. What each plan includes is on Plans; a plan marked "Coming soon" there can't be bought yet. If free answers get very busy in a month, the free plan's daily answers may drop until the month ends.
