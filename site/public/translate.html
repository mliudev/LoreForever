Translate Lore Forever

Help Lore Forever speak your language. Open the translation dashboard to read the English original beside your language, fix a line, or add a translation. You don't need to code or install anything.

Help translate

  1. Sign in with your Google account. It takes a minute.
  2. Open the translation dashboard and pick your language. Each English line has a box under it for yours. You can improve existing translations or fill in missing ones.
  3. Type and wait for Saved. If a translation already reads correctly after you compare it with the English, press Looks right to save your check of that line. Your work is credited if you like.

You don't have to finish anything: every line you translate is used, and the rest stays in English until someone gets to it. Start with the interface and the zones. Questions? Ask on Discord.

Rather work offline? Download your language's kit below and edit it in a text editor. When you're done, drop the kit (the zip, its folder, or just the .json files you changed) on Upload your edited kit in the dashboard. It shows what would change before saving anything, and every string you filled in or changed becomes your edit, as if you'd typed it there. Strings whose English changed since you downloaded the kit are skipped and listed, so download a fresh kit for those. Can't upload? Share the kit as a link with the submission form instead.

Check one script, then record it

  1. In the voice studio, open a line's text. Its entry ID and English original identify the source; Check or correct translation opens the matching entry in your language. The fields used by that script are marked.
  2. Compare those fields with the English and fix anything that reads wrong. Looks right means you checked that line's wording; it does not approve a recording or the whole language.
  3. Wait for Saved, then return to the same recording line. The studio reads its current script, which updates separately from your saved edits. If you changed the wording, refresh the script and wait until it shows your correction before recording. A personal translation test pack does not change the studio script.

You can work on one entry at a time; the whole language does not need to reach 100% first. Existing recordings are kept when text changes; text or the default voice covers lines that do not have a current recording.

Which names should I use?

Use the names shown in your game client in the language you are translating: places, people, factions and quests. If your French client calls Stormwind Hurlevent, use that name; if it keeps Stormwind, keep it. Check the map, quest log or character name when unsure. Custom names without a localized client name stay as they are. Correct the written script before recording a different name.

See your translation in game

Your saved edits reach every player when they go into a language update. Until then, a test pack lets you see them in your own game.

  1. Save your edits in the dashboard, or upload your edited kit there.
  2. Press Download my test pack there. Each language has its own.
  3. Unzip it into World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns. It's the same folder every time: to update it, unzip the new one over it.
  4. Keep the language pack installed if you have it. The test pack sits on top of it (it also works alone).
  5. In game, type /reload so the game finds it (no restart needed).
  6. In Lore Forever's Options, under Language, pick your language.
What's in the test pack?

Your saved edits in that language that haven't gone into an update yet. Lines you cleared, and edits that weren't used, are left out. If the English of a line changed since you saved it, it's left out too and the game shows the current translation instead; the dashboard says how many were left out. The pack is only for testing on your own computer: once your edits are in an update, you can delete the LoreForever_LangTest folder.

Nothing changed in game?
  • Restart WoW once: that always finds a new add-on, even if /reload didn't.
  • Check the folder isn't nested twice: AddOns\LoreForever_LangTest_deDE\LoreForever_LangTest_deDE.toc (with your language's code), not AddOns\LoreForever_LangTest_deDE\LoreForever_LangTest_deDE\....
  • Check Options › Language. After you log in, a chat line says how many of your lines are in use.

When does a language come to the game?

Saved edits are imported into the language source, merged, then included in a released language pack. Saving or checking a line does not release it immediately. The coverage percentage says how much text is available. Translations are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, like the lore text they translate, and every translator is credited.
