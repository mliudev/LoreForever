Translate Lore Forever

Help Lore Forever speak your language. You don't need to code or install anything: the text comes as simple files with the English next to each line, and you write your language beside it.

Help translate

  1. Sign in with your Google account. It takes a minute.
  2. Open the translation dashboard and pick your language. Each English line has a box under it for yours. A language with a draft already has text to check and fix; a new one starts from the English.
  3. Type. Your work saves by itself, and goes into your language's next update, with your name in the credits if you like.

You don't have to finish anything: every line you translate is used, and the rest stays in English until someone gets to it. Start with the interface and the zones. Questions? Ask on Discord, where native speakers also review each language.

Rather work offline? Download your language's kit below and edit it in a text editor. When you're done, drop the kit (the zip, its folder, or just the .json files you changed) on Upload your edited kit in the dashboard. It shows what would change before saving anything, and every string you filled in or changed becomes your edit, as if you'd typed it there. Strings whose English changed since you downloaded the kit are skipped and listed, so download a fresh kit for those. Can't upload? Share the kit as a link with the submission form instead.

See your translation in game

Your saved edits reach every player when they go into a language update. Until then, a test pack lets you see them in your own game.

  1. Save your edits in the dashboard, or upload your edited kit there.
  2. Press Download my test pack there. Each language has its own.
  3. Unzip it into World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns. It's the same folder every time: to update it, unzip the new one over it.
  4. Keep the language pack installed if you have it. The test pack sits on top of it (it also works alone).
  5. In game, type /reload so the game finds it (no restart needed).
  6. In Lore Forever's Options, under Language, pick your language.
What's in the test pack?

Your saved edits in that language that haven't gone into an update yet. Lines you cleared, and edits that weren't used, are left out. If the English of a line changed since you saved it, it's left out too and the game shows the current translation instead; the dashboard says how many were left out. The pack is only for testing on your own computer: once your edits are in an update, you can delete the LoreForever_LangTest folder.

Nothing changed in game?
  • Restart WoW once: that always finds a new add-on, even if /reload didn't.
  • Check the folder isn't nested twice: AddOns\LoreForever_LangTest_deDE\LoreForever_LangTest_deDE.toc (with your language's code), not AddOns\LoreForever_LangTest_deDE\LoreForever_LangTest_deDE\....
  • Check Options › Language. After you log in, a chat line says how many of your lines are in use.

Languages

How much of Lore Forever each language has so far. Press 👍 for the languages you'd like to play in: it helps us decide where to start.

When does a language come to the game?

When enough of it is translated and native speakers have read it through. We'll announce each one on Discord. Translations are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, like the lore text they translate, and every translator is credited.
