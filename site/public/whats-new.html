What's new

Lore Forever updates almost every day. Here's what each version brought, newest first.

0.30.0

October 27, 2026

All 8 changes in 0.30.0
  • Books are read aloud: open a book, letter or plaque and the narrator reads each page as you turn it, with Listen to hear it again. They come with the More quests (levels 40+) voice pack.
  • Rewritten lore, read aloud: the rewritten places, people, items and quests tell their new stories in both narrators' voices, hidden passages too.
  • Willo remembers you: tell her to remember that you main a hunter and she keeps it in mind, with Undo under her answer. Settings › Privacy & sharing lists everything she remembers, each with Forget.
  • Romanian and Swedish: Lore Forever in Română (translated by Indurin) and Svenska, as language packs.
  • A simpler downloads page: your language and narrator are picked for you. Choose The add-on, Storyteller or Everything, and Setup comes ticked for what you picked.
  • Which voice pack am I missing? Options > Narration voices says whether you have all of a narrator's stories or names the project to install, and a quest's play button names the pack for its level.
  • Quest dialogue by level: three downloads, 1–25, 26–50 and 51–60. Have the quest pack? Add 26–50 and 51–60 to keep hearing quests past level 25.
  • Female narrator on CurseForge: installing her brings her Horde lands too, and her packs are named narrator first so they sit together. Item stories now come with Quest Stories 40+.
