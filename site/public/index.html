    Hear the story of where you are

    Every zone is narrated, and so are the places and people inside it, Alliance and Horde alike. The main download includes their stories, dungeon bosses and recorded answers to the questions new players ask first.

    • Plays as you arrive. Reach a zone or place and its story starts by itself. It waits for the fight to end and never cuts off what you're listening to.
    • Your quest's backstory. Open its Lore button to hear Lore Forever's recorded story. Quest dialogue is a separate optional download, off by default.
    • A player with a queue. What's playing, Back, Play and Next sit at the bottom of the panel. Queue up stories with +, and the player floats on screen when you close the panel.
    • On flights, too. Turn on flight narration and each zone's story plays as you fly over it. Everything you hear is a Lore Forever narration, never your game's robot voice.

    Want a different narrator? Install as many voices as you like from the Downloads page and put them in your order: each narration plays from the first voice that has it. Or lend your own voice.

    Ask about what's around you

    The panel already knows your zone, your quests and your target, and suggests questions to start with. Type your own and matching questions drop down as you go. Every answer ends with follow-ups, so you can keep pulling the thread.

    • "Who is this guy?" means your target.
    • Typos in names are fine. You don't need to spell every NPC right.
    • No made-up answers. If it isn't sure, it asks "Did you mean..." instead.
    • Names are links. The places, people and events an answer mentions are clickable, and Back and Forward retrace what you followed. Quests you haven't picked up stay plain, so nothing is spoiled.

    New: your journey, on the map

    Journey keeps a timeline of each play session, with Quests, Fights and Milestones to filter by, and a map of the world beside it. Hover a moment and the map shows where it happened and the road you took there. Click anything with a story to hear it in the chat. It all stays on your PC.

    • Welcome back. The first chat after you log in sums up your last session: where you went, the quests you finished and the people you met.
    • Five languages available. English is included; Deutsch, Français, Español (España) and Português (Brasil) are separate text-language downloads. Once installed, they follow those game clients, or pick one in Options › Language. Help translate into yours.
    • Your voices, your order. Install several voices and choose which comes first; a story keeps one voice for its questions.
    • Search the long lists. Type to find a story in the Library instead of scrolling.

    Zone lore, quest lore and storyline for WoW Forever

    Ever finish a quest chain and realize you have no idea why you just killed forty gnolls? Lore Forever gives you the story behind what you're doing, without alt-tabbing to a wiki.

    The first time you log in, pick a key to open it, like M for the map. Hover over an NPC and press it to read about them. The panel already knows where you are and what you're working on: it suggests questions about your zone and your quests, and you can type your own. Answers link to related people, places and events, so you can keep pulling the thread as far as you like.

    Lore wherever you look

    • Tooltips. NPCs and mobs get a one-line story. Items tell you "Needed for your quest" or "Starts a quest", and only ever name quests you already have, so nothing is spoiled.
    • Quests. A Lore button on the quest dialog and in the quest log opens the story behind that quest.
    • Zone hints. Entering a zone puts a clickable question in chat. Turn it off with /lore nudge.
    • Always in reach. Your key, the minimap book, the add-on compartment, or /lore.

    Dungeon primers

    Walk into a dungeon and chat links a primer: why you're there, the bosses in order, and what beating the final boss means (tagged as a spoiler, so it's your call). Beat a boss and chat links their story.

    Your journey

    Lore Forever remembers what your character has done: places, people, quests, foes and books, on a timeline and a map. The first chat after you log in recaps your last session. It stays on your PC, and Options › Remember my journey turns it off.

    True to Forever

    Forever has its own timeline, so Lore Forever sticks to it: no story from retail's later expansions, and where Forever and Classic disagree, Forever wins.

    Free and offline

    Everything is inside the add-on. No account, no extra app, nothing to sign up for.

    Install

    With the Windows installer

    1. Click Download for Windows at the top of this page and run LoreForever-Setup.exe. It's signed; Windows shows the publisher as Mei Liu.
    2. If Windows shows a SmartScreen prompt, click More info, then Run anyway. (New publishers can see this for a few weeks.)
    3. It finds your World of Warcraft folder and installs into _classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns. No admin rights needed. To update later, run a newer installer.
    4. Start WoW, or type /reload if it's already running.

    With the zip

    1. Click zip at the top of this page to get LoreForever.zip.
    2. Open your WoW folder, then _classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns (create Interface\AddOns if it isn't there). On Windows the default is C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns.
    3. Unzip so you end up with ...\AddOns\LoreForever\LoreForever.toc. A doubled folder like AddOns\LoreForever\LoreForever\ won't load.
    4. Start WoW, or type /reload if it's already running.

    With the CurseForge app

    Lore Forever is on CurseForge, so the app can install it and keep it updated. Extra narrator voices and quest dialogue are separate CurseForge packs, listed on the Downloads page.

    1. In the CurseForge app, open World of Warcraft and pick your Forever install. If it isn't listed, update the app (Forever support came in September) and add your _classic_beta_ folder, or use the installer or zip above.
    2. Search for Lore Forever and click Install.
    3. Start the game. On the character screen, open AddOns and make sure Lore Forever is ticked.

    “Game was not found or not supported” means the app can't see your Forever install yet: do step 1, or use the installer or zip above.

    Extra narrator voices

    Lore Forever comes with the male narrator. Choose extra voices and quest dialogue on the Downloads page, with CurseForge packs or zips for manual installs. Install the packs you want, type /reload in game and choose your voice order in Options. Step by step.

    Quest Dialogue packs are separate optional downloads, outside the main download and story bundles. Quest dialogue stays off until you turn on Speak quest dialogue in Options; this also enables its play button on the quest window. Skip these packs if Spoken, Chatty Little NPC or another add-on already speaks your quests. Lore Forever still narrates its own stories and recorded answers.

    On your first login it asks you to pick a key for the panel. You can change it later with /lore key.

    Not showing up? On the character screen, click AddOns and tick Load out of date AddOns.

    Known limits

    • Above level 30: zones get place lore (the zone, subzones and notable characters), not quest-by-quest lore yet. Mount Hyjal isn't covered yet.
    • Questions: it answers from its built-in lore library. When it can't find a good match it says so or asks "Did you mean...". Asking with a name ("Who is Hogger?") works best.
    • Forever-only quests: some quests new in Forever may show "(no lore yet)".
    • Settings may reset: the Forever beta has a known bug where add-on settings don't always load back after you log out. That's on the game side.
    • Beta client: built for build 1.60.1. Game patches can break things, so please report problems.
