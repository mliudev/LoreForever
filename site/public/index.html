The wisp

Ask out loud, and the world answers

Willo is an app for your Windows PC that rides along while you play. Hold your talk key, ask, and hear her answer.

Ask out loud

Hold your talk key and ask about anything in Azeroth. She answers in her own voice.

Characters answer

Target anyone, from King Magni to an innkeeper, and they answer as themselves.

She knows your journey

Your race, your class and where you've been. Ask what you were up to, and she recaps it.

Chat in game

Click Willo to chat right there, or open her window beside your journey.
