    How it works

    1. Apply below with a link to your channel. We read every application ourselves, and creators we partner with sign our Creator Partnership Agreement.
    2. Get your link, like loreforeverwow.com/w/yourname, your code, like YOURNAME, a free Champion plan, and a dashboard on this page with your clicks, sign-ups, paying viewers and earnings.
    3. Your audience saves 15% on their first month. Your link takes it off at checkout, and anyone can type your code there instead.
    4. Earn 60% to 90% of each new subscriber's first payment. The more people you bring in during your term, the bigger your share.
    5. Get paid after each 30-day cycle, by PayPal or bank wire, once a commission is 30 days old and you're owed at least $10.

    Creator terms

    Creators we partner with sign our Creator Partnership Agreement. In short:

    • Term. 90 days from your start date, as three 30-day payout cycles. It doesn't renew by itself; we can agree to extend it in writing.
    • Your free plan. Champion, free for your term, for your own use, with the same limits as a paying account. It's a benefit from us, so say so when you talk about Willo.
    • What counts. A Conversion is someone who starts a paid plan for the first time through your link or code, during your term, whose first payment went through and wasn't refunded. You, accounts you control, and duplicate or fake accounts don't count.
    • What your audience gets. 15% off their first month of a plan, once per account, with your link or your code.
    • What you earn. Your share (the table above) of what each Conversion paid in their first payment, after the discount and without sales tax or VAT. We cover the payment fees. Renewals, upgrades and later payments don't earn a commission.
    • Payouts. In US dollars, by PayPal or bank wire, within 7 days after each 30-day cycle ends. Each commission can be paid 30 days after its payment, and we pay once you're owed at least $10; less carries over to the next payout, after your term too. We can't pay you until you've given us your payout details.
    • Refunds and chargebacks. We take that commission back, from your next payout if it was paid already.
    • Taxes. Yours. US creators send us a Form W-9, and everyone else a Form W-8BEN.
    • Say it's a partnership. Every post, video or stream about Willo says, at the start and in words people can read or hear, that it's a partnership, that you may earn a commission and that you got free access. Say what you really think.
    • Not allowed. Using your own code for yourself, fake or duplicate accounts, paying people to subscribe, spam, ads that bid on our names, look-alike accounts, saying Willo is made or endorsed by Blizzard, or false claims about Willo. Conversions from any of these don't count.
    • Ending. Either of us can end it at any time. From then your link and code stop earning and your free plan ends; everything you earned before is still paid on the usual schedule.
    • Our names. You may use the Lore Forever and Willo names, and screenshots of them, to show Willo to your audience. These terms sit alongside the site's Terms.
