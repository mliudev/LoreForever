    How it works

    1. Apply below with a link to your channel. We read every application ourselves.
    2. Get your link, like loreforeverwow.com/w/yourname, your code, like YOURNAME, and a dashboard on this page with your clicks, sign-ups and earnings.
    3. Your audience saves 15% on their first month. Your link takes it off at checkout, and anyone can type your code there instead.
    4. Earn 60% of each subscriber's first month. When someone who came through your link or your code starts a plan, you get that share of what they pay for their first month, before tax. Later months are ours.
    5. Get paid monthly. A commission can be paid 30 days after the payment it comes from, once you're owed at least $10.

    Creator terms

    • Who can join. Creators we approve. We can say no, and we can end the program, or your part in it, at any time; we tell you first, and what you earned before that is still paid, unless it broke these terms.
    • What counts. Someone counts for you when they open your link, then make their Lore Forever account or connect Willo for the first time within 30 days, or when they type your code at checkout for their first plan. People who already used Willo don't count, and neither does anyone who came through another link first.
    • What your audience gets. 15% off their first month of a plan, once per account, with your link or your code. It doesn't add to other offers: while another offer is on, they get that one instead, and you still earn on what they pay.
    • What you earn. 60% of what they pay for their first month of a plan, before tax: their first full month, and any days or upgrade they paid for before it ends. Later months, and anything else they buy, are ours.
    • Refunds and disputes. If a payment is refunded or disputed, its commission is taken back, from your next payout if it was paid already.
    • Not allowed. Signing up yourself or accounts you control, paying for someone with your own card, ads that bid on our names, spam, saying Willo is made or endorsed by Blizzard, or promising viewers money back. Commissions from any of these don't count.
    • Say it's a partnership. Tell your audience your link earns you money, wherever your platform or the law asks you to (in the US, the FTC's endorsement rules).
    • Payouts. Once a month, in US dollars, through Stripe once you've set up payouts on this page, or by hand until then. A commission can be paid 30 days after its payment; we pay once you're owed at least $10, and less carries over. Taxes on what you earn are yours.
    • Changes. We may change the rate or these terms. A change only applies to people who join through your link after we've told you about it.
    • Our names. You may use the Lore Forever and Willo names, and screenshots of them, to show Willo to your audience. These terms sit alongside the site's Terms.
