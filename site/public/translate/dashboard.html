Try your edits in game
  • The test pack holds the edits you've saved in this language that aren't in a language update yet, so you can see them in game right away.
  • Unzip it into Interface\AddOns, next to LoreForever. Restart WoW the first time, so the game finds it.
  • In Lore Forever's Options, pick your language (Automatic works if your game is in that language).
  • After more edits, download it again, unzip it over the old folder and type /reload.
  • Lines whose English changed since you saved them are left out. Once your edits are in a language update, you can delete the LoreForever_LangTest folder.

More about test packs, and what to do if nothing changes

How it works
  • Needs work lists what still needs a person, most-read first: lines with no translation, drafts nobody has checked, lines players reported, and lines whose English changed.
  • Type your language in the box under each English line. It saves by itself when you pause or move on.
  • A draft that already reads right? Press Looks right on the line, or on the whole entry.
  • Keep codes like %s and |cff00ff00 ... |r where the English has them. Keywords and search phrases are lists: separate the items with |.
  • Names of places, people and quests should match what your game client calls them.
  • What you save goes into your language's next update. Clear a box to take your edit back before then.
