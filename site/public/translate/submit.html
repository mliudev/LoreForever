Send a translation

Translated or fixed part of Lore Forever in the kit? The quickest way is to drop your kit on Upload your edited kit in the translation dashboard: you see what changes, and it's in your test pack right away. Can't upload there? Put the files you changed in a shared folder and send us the link, or paste a few fixes below. We'll check them, add them to your language and credit you.

Don't have the files yet? Download the kit for your language. Questions? Ask on Discord.

Sign in first

Sending a translation needs a Lore Forever account, so it's credited to you and we can reach you. It takes a minute with your Google account. Easier still: once you're signed in, you can translate right in your browser.
