What we keep, and how to delete it

From Google: your account ID, email address and name (to create your account and reach you about your feedback and what you contribute). Then what you type into your profile here, the languages you translate and the edits you make on the translation dashboard, and a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days. We don't email you otherwise, and we don't share or sell any of it.

Delete my account (on this page once you're signed in) removes all of that at once, along with what's listed under your name on the site and the recordings you uploaded here. Recordings you sent us for review, and the narrator release you agreed to for them, are kept as the release describes; ask us if you want them removed too. Feedback you sent is kept, without your email. Translations already added to a language stay in it, under CC BY-SA, without your name.
