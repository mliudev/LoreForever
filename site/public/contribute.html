Share the Forever text you've seen

Forever rewrote old quests and added new ones, and Lore Forever learns them from players. The add-on keeps the quest, gossip and book text you see in game. Share it here and it goes into a coming update, so every player gets those stories too.

Which zones and quests still need text: see the progress so far. Everyone who helps is on the contributors page.

      Got a link or code from the game?

      The Contribute button on a quest, gossip or book window gives you a link. Opening it brings you here; you can also paste it.

      What's sent and kept

      • The text itself, with which quest, NPC or book it's from, the game's language and build, and the race, class and gender of the character who saw it (some lines change with them). Your character's name is taken out in the add-on before anything is saved, and again here.
      • Your name for the credits, only if you give one, or your Lore Forever account's name if you're signed in and show it.
      • Never your file. It's read in your browser; only the lines are sent.
      • Your IP address is never stored. A one-way hash of it, salted and changed every day, stops floods (10 uploads an hour). Upload records drop even that after 30 days.
      • Lines become part of Lore Forever's text, which is free for every player. Each upload gets a receipt page showing what happened to its lines.

      Shared text helps every Lore Forever player. Spam is removed; nothing else is reviewed by hand.
