Plans

We're connected, friend! Chat with me free, or pick a plan: ask me out loud and I'll answer you aloud for hours of play.

Lore Forever is free and stays free: the add-on, every narration pack and every language, and Willo, the companion, to chat with you about the world: 50 answers a day, out loud when you ask out loud, and your first days with everything she can do. With a plan she does more: unlimited answers, her finest voice on every answer, she checks the web and sees your game, and keeps your goals; on Champion you pick her voice and characters answer in their own; Legend adds her richer voice, her smarter mind and the Voices of Azeroth, and gets new features first. Typed questions get text answers.
