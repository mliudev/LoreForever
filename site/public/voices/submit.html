Send your recordings

Recorded some narrations? The quickest way is to drop a zip or folder of them on the upload page: each file is checked and goes to its line, and you can hear them in game right away. Can't upload there? Put the files in a shared folder and send us the link here. We'll listen to every one, build your pack and get back to you.

New to this? Start with the narrator guide and the clip list. Questions? Ask on Discord.

Sign in first

Sending a voice needs a Lore Forever account, so your voice is tied to you and you can edit its page. It takes a minute with your Google account.
